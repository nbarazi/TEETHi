## 3. Findings

### Medium

**B1-01. A pay-later collection leaves an older card PaymentIntent open in Stripe.**
- Invoice 5 ($180) had a card PaymentIntent `pi_3UTjhaRme4hzZ7dC1yMbVs0k` from an earlier attempt. Staff then recorded a $180 collection (reconcile to `paid` with `amount_collected`). The invoice is paid, but the intent is still `requires_payment_method` in Stripe for $180, and the dashboard lists it as "Initiated".
- If anything confirms that intent later, the patient is charged twice. The P3 path already cancels open intents when the total changes; `InvoiceService::reconcile()` does not.
- Fix: when reconcile records a collection or changes the status to `paid`, `cancelled` or `refunded`, cancel the invoice's open gateway intents, as the total-change path does. This is the "stale PaymentIntent cleanup" item in roadmap §4.3, now with a concrete path.
- **Fixed and merged (TEETHi-Backend `4f8e9d1`):** `reconcile()` cancels open card intents when staff record a collection or set Paid, Cancelled or Refunded, and `cancelUnpaidForAppointment()` does the same when a visit is cancelled or declined (the same gap on the visit-cancel path). An intent Stripe refuses to cancel is left for its webhook. 884 tests, PHPStan max, Pint and Rector clean. Checked live: a $100 collection on invoice 3 cancelled its open intent in Stripe (`canceled`, `abandoned`). Invoice 5's old intent was cancelled by hand.

**B1-05. A cancelled invoice can be paid.** On the phone, invoice 4 (status Cancelled, $100) showed "Pay now", and `POST /patient/appointments/4/payment` created a $100 card PaymentIntent (`pi_3UalKLRme4hzZ7dC0dxVy5dE`, payment 20). The tester closed the sheet, so nothing was charged, but finishing it would have charged a cancelled visit.
- Backend: `AppointmentPolicy::pay()` only checks the patient owns the visit, and `AppointmentController::pay()` only checks the amount against `remaining_amount`, which a cancelled invoice keeps. Refuse `cancelled`, `refunded` and `paid` invoices (`409`).
- Patient app: `record_detail_screen.dart` shows the Pay button whenever `!invoice.isPaid`. Show it only for `pending` and `partially_paid`.
- **Fixed and merged to `master` (pushed 2026-11-12):** TEETHi-Backend `f553122` on `fix/b1-05-pay-closed-invoice` (`409` for paid, refunded and cancelled invoices; 879 tests, PHPStan max, Pint, Rector clean; checked live: appointment 5 / invoice 4 now gets `409`). TEETHi-Patient-App `e20fcac` on `fix/sheet-nav-bar` (`Invoice.isPayable`; Pay button and amount due only when payable; Cancelled and Refunded badges; 217 tests pass). The stray intent `pi_3UalKLRme4hzZ7dC0dxVy5dE` was cancelled in Stripe.

### Low

**B1-06. The app's payment-method sheet hid its Confirm button under the Android navigation bar.** Same cause as the Reason sheet fixed in `85c3f37`: sheets draw edge to edge. Fixed in TEETHi-Patient-App `e96d4b9` (merged to `master`) for the payment sheet, the review sheet and the three profile sheets, with one shared `sheetBottomPadding()` helper.

**B1-07. A completed visit's call says "join from 15 minutes before".** When the patient's token renewal is refused after the visit is completed, the app shows the backend's `tele.call_not_open` text: "This call can't be joined now. Join from 15 minutes before the visit, or once your dentist has started it." For a completed or cancelled visit it should say the visit has ended. **Fixed and merged (TEETHi-Backend `2d23774`):** `Appointment::isCallOver()` (completed, cancelled, declined, or past the call window) now answers "This visit has ended, so its call is closed." (`tele.call_ended`, English and Arabic) on both call-token routes and the dentist's Start. 879 tests, PHPStan max, Pint and Rector clean; checked live on visit 27.

**B1-02. A refunded invoice shows its full amount as Remaining.** Invoice 1 (paid $40, refunded $40, status Refunded) shows Remaining $100.00 in the dashboard. The balance is arithmetically right, but on a Refunded invoice it reads as money owed. Either show Remaining as "—" for Refunded and Cancelled invoices or decide that Refunded means the debt stands. Needs a product call.

**B1-03. A cancelled payment sheet is labelled "Failed".** Closing the sheet (intent canceled) stores the payment as `failed`, so staff see "Failed" next to an attempt the patient just abandoned. A `canceled` status or label would be clearer. Old `pay_later` rows in `initiated` also stay on paid invoices forever (invoices 1, 5, 20).

**B1-04. The receipt PDF is 858 KB for one page.** dompdf embeds the whole font. Subsetting the font (`enable_font_subsetting`) or a core font would cut it to a few KB, which matters on mobile data.

### Noted, already tracked

- `charge.refunded` is forwarded by the CLI (it is in the `stripe listen` list) but the backend ignores it, so a refund made in Stripe's own dashboard is not mirrored. Roadmap §4.3 / SEC S7.
